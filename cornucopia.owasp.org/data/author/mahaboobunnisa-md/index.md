# Mahaboobunnisa Md

Mahaboobunnisa Md is a Google Summer of Code 2026 contributor to OWASP Cornucopia. She designed and built ThreatSutra, an automated AI-assisted pipeline that transforms OWASP Threat Dragon data-flow diagrams into validated, developer-ready security requirements and GitHub Issues. Her work bridges the gap between threat modeling and actionable development tasks using Gemini 2.5 Flash.

The OWASP Foundation is her first open-source organization, and Cornucopia is her very first open-source project contribution. Through building ThreatSutra, she developed a strong interest in the intersection of artificial intelligence, threat modeling, and secure software development. She is incredibly proud of what she has learned and intends to keep contributing to the OWASP community long into the future.
